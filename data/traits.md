# Career-inspired player traits

`dist/traits.js` owns 25 trait definitions and a curated set of 28 signature profiles, keyed by the catalogue's canonical person identity. Each profile includes its source URL and a short rationale, available in the game's Player traits guide. Signatures apply to every edition of the same person, including editions linked through historical identity crosswalks. The modern Tigran L. Petrosian is not the former world champion.

The evidence concerns recurring playing preferences and habits, rather than a single famous game or tournament. A signature highlights one aspect of a player's career; it does not claim that their style was static or limited to that aspect. Some published material is a player's own assessment, and some is a grandmaster's analysis or a FIDE career profile. Nakamura's citation is a print interview: Karsten Müller and Raymund Stolze, *Fighting Chess with Hikaru Nakamura* (Edition Olms, 2012), chapter 6, p. 138, interview by IM Georgios Souleidis in July 2011. Its URL links the publisher record, not the interview text.

**All numerical effects, tradeoffs and simulated match conditions are fictional balance rules.** A negative modifier is not a biographical claim about a player's weakness. Traits never modify a published source rating, catalogue ranking, price or national roster. There are no simulated chess moves: opening theory, sharp play, quiet maneuvering, technical endings and time pressure are abstract conditions used to activate different abilities.

Unprofiled players have a 25% chance of no trait and otherwise an equal chance of each trait. Those rolls are game data, not claims about the real person. Roll once for each appearance in a draft offer, including its disabled candidates, and retain the chosen player's result through the game. A new offer may roll a different trait for an unprofiled edition. Famous signatures never reroll. Tournament opponents receive their own seeded rolls at the beginning and keep them for the tournament.

The match resolver evaluates both sides' abilities simultaneously. Positive Elo bonuses are capped at 40 per side; defensive counters only reduce positive bonuses. Draw changes are percentage-point changes, combined from both players and bounded to keep probabilities valid. They change result variance while preserving the expected score implied by effective Elo and color. Source Elo, fatigue loss and trait modifiers are displayed separately. Both user and opponent players follow the same trait and fatigue rules.

Trait rules have a separate version marker from the SQL catalogue. Saved assignments must include an explicit `null` for no trait; missing or unknown traits and altered signature traits are rejected. Increment the trait rules marker and deliberately handle save compatibility when changing rules or assignments.
